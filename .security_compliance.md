## Known Limitations (Documented)

These are intentional design choices, not vulnerabilities:

1. **No passphrase recovery** - By design for zero-knowledge security
2. **Physical access risk** - Inherent limitation (applies to all systems)
3. **Memory dumps** - May contain decrypted data during active sessions
4. **Side-channel attacks** - Not explicitly mitigated (future work)

All limitations are documented in SECURITY.md and SECURITY_AUDIT.md.
